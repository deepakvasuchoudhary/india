# Chronica Imperii Indici: India's Colonial Past from Start to End (1498 – 1961)

An interactive, museum-grade scrollytelling website and geographical chronicle that charts the 451-year history of European colonial rule in India—from Vasco da Gama's landing in Calicut in 1498 to the armed liberation of Goa in 1961.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Synchronized Scrollytelling & Interactive Cartography**
   - As the reader scrolls through the chronological narrative, the Leaflet map smoothly flies to the exact historical location (coordinates, custom zoom levels, active marker pulse).
   - **Custom Map Markers**: Unique glowing SVG pins categorizing Battles ⚔️, Treaties 📜, Famines 🌾, Rebellions 🔥, Trade/Maritime 🚢, Imperial Decrees 🏛️, Freedom Movements 🇮🇳, and Liberation 🕊️.
   - **Historical Route Overlays**: Visualized paths for the 1930 Dandi Salt March (Sabarmati to Dandi) and the 1857 War of Independence Epicenter Axis.
   - **Dynamic Territory Polygons**: Toggle historical territorial layers for 1700 (Mughal zenith), 1765 (EIC Diwani), 1805 (Wellesley's Expansions), 1857 (Eve of Revolt), 1947 (Radcliffe Partition Line), and 1961 (Goa Liberation).
   - **On-Map HUD (Heads-Up Display)**: Real-time readout of the active event's date, era, coordinates, and exact location.

2. **63 Chronological Milestones (Every Major Event Explained)**
   - **Chapter 1 (1498–1744)**: Vasco da Gama, Battle of Diu, Conquest of Goa, EIC Royal Charter 1600, Sir Thomas Roe at Jahangir's court, Fort St. George (Madras), Bombay dowry lease, Job Charnock & Fort William (Calcutta), Farrukhsiyar's 1717 Farman, Battle of Colachel (Marthanda Varma crushes Dutch VOC).
   - **Chapter 2 (1744–1765)**: Carnatic Wars, Siege of Arcot, Siraj-ud-Daulah takes Calcutta, Battle of Plassey (1757, Clive and Mir Jafar's betrayal), Battle of Wandiwash (1760), Battle of Buxar (1764), Treaty of Allahabad (1765, Diwani rights & Dual Government).
   - **Chapter 3 (1765–1849)**: 1770 Great Bengal Famine (10M dead), Warren Hastings Regulating Act, Four Anglo-Mysore Wars (Hyder Ali, Tipu Sultan's rocketry, Seringapatam 1799), Three Anglo-Maratha Wars (Treaty of Salbai, Battle of Assaye, 1818 abolition of Peshwaship), Lord Wellesley's Subsidiary Alliance, Rani Chennamma of Kittur (1824), First Anglo-Afghan disaster, Napier's conquest of Sindh (1843), Anglo-Sikh Wars (Battles of Sobraon, Chillianwala, Gujrat 1849, fall of Punjab, Duleep Singh deposed, Koh-i-Noor seized).
   - **Chapter 4 (1849–1858)**: Lord Dalhousie's Doctrine of Lapse (Jhansi, Satara, Nagpur), Annexation of Awadh (1856), Santhal Hul, Mangal Pandey at Barrackpore, Meerut mutiny & march to Delhi, Bahadur Shah Zafar crowned Emperor, Nana Saheb & Tatya Tope in Kanpur, Rani Lakshmibai of Jhansi, Begum Hazrat Mahal in Lucknow, Veer Kunwar Singh in Bihar, Sacking of Delhi & Hodson's murders, Queen Victoria's Proclamation 1858 (Crown Rule begins).
   - **Chapter 5 (1858–1905)**: Indigo Revolt (Nil Darpan), Orissa Famine 1866, Dadabhai Naoroji unmasks the Drain of Wealth, Great Famine 1876–78 (5.5M–10M dead) & Lord Lytton's lavish 1877 Delhi Durbar banquet, Founding of the Indian National Congress (1885), Twin Famines & Bombay Plague 1896–1900, Birsa Munda's Ulgulan.
   - **Chapter 6 (1905–1930)**: Lord Curzon Partitions Bengal (1905), Swadeshi & Boycott Movement, Surat Split 1907 (Lal-Bal-Pal & Tilak's roar 'Swaraj is my birthright'), Alipore bomb trial & Khudiram Bose, Morley-Minto communal electorates, WWI Indian soldiers, Mahatma Gandhi returns from South Africa, Rowlatt Act 1919, Jallianwala Bagh Massacre (Gen. Dyer kills 1,000+), Non-Cooperation & Chauri Chaura, Kakori Train Action (1925), Simon Commission protests & death of Lala Lajpat Rai, Saunders shot by Bhagat Singh & Rajguru, Central Assembly bombing ('To make the deaf hear'), Lahore Session 1929 & Purna Swaraj tricolor unfurling, Dandi Salt March 1930, Chittagong Armoury Raid (Surya Sen & Pritilata Waddedar).
   - **Chapter 7 (1930–1946)**: Martyrdom of Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, Sukhdev (March 23, 1931), Poona Pact 1932 (Ambedkar & Gandhi), India dragged into WWII without consent (1939), Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose's Great Escape (1941), Quit India Movement 1942 ('Do or Die', Aruna Asaf Ali, parallel governments), The 1943 Bengal Famine (Churchill's denial policy, 3.8M dead), Azad Hind Fauj & Rani of Jhansi women's combat regiment, Battles of Kohima & Imphal, INA Red Fort Trials (Sahgal, Dhillon, Shahnawaz), Royal Indian Navy (RIN) Mutiny of Feb 1946.
   - **Chapter 8 (1946–1961)**: Mountbatten Plan & Radcliffe boundary demarcation, Midnight 'Tryst with Destiny' (August 15, 1947), Human tragedy of Partition (15M displaced, 1M dead), Sardar Patel integrates 565 Princely States (Operation Polo 1948 in Hyderabad), Transfer of French Enclaves (Pondicherry 1954), and Operation Vijay (December 18–19, 1961) liberating Portuguese Goa, Daman, and Diu, concluding 451 years of colonial rule.

3. **Specialized Explorer Dossiers (Interactive Modals)**
   - **⚔️ Major Wars & Battles**: 16 wars cataloged with theaters, belligerents, commanders, key battles, tactical breakthroughs, and geopolitical outcomes.
   - **📜 Historic Treaties Archive**: 13 landmark treaties with signatories, locations, key clauses, and imperial consequences.
   - **🌾 The Great Famines Register**: 10 catastrophic famines analyzing death tolls and Victorian/Churchillian laissez-faire policies, forced food exports, and starvation labor rations.
   - **👑 Dramatis Personae**: 43 historical biographies of Indian royalty, British viceroys/generals, and freedom fighters with instant event filtering.
   - **💰 $45 Trillion Economic Drain**: Detailed breakdown of Prof. Utsa Patnaik's study and the 5 primary mechanisms of imperial resource extraction.

4. **Atmospheric Sound & Presentation Features**
   - **Auto-Play Tour Mode**: Hands-free presentation tour cycling through major historical milestones with smooth camera pans and progress tracker.
   - **Web Audio API Ambient Synthesizer**: Subtle tanpura/drone soundscape, battle drums, and UI interaction clicks with zero external audio file dependencies.

## 🚀 How to Run

Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser, or launch a local development server:

```bash
# Using Python
python3 -m http.server 8080

# Or using Node
npx serve .
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8080` in your web browser.
